# Console first-run audit

A clarity review of what a new user sees in the management console ([console.md](../console.md)) right after installation: the sign-in page, Gateway control (G-01) with the first-run checklist (FR-8.31), empty and error states (FR-8.29), help (FR-8.25), and keyboard use (FR-8.32, FR-8.33). It was made on 2026-10-18 against a fresh Standalone Gateway on macOS (the Playwright global setup in `console/e2e/gateway.ts`), signed in as the Administrator and as an Approver, in Chromium. The rules applied are [console-style.md](../console-style.md), SRS Appendix B (message style) and Appendix C (roles); the writing and visual guides being added as `docs/design/writing.md` and `docs/design/visual.md` cover the same ground in more detail.

Severity: **high** means a new user cannot tell what to do next or misreads the security state; **medium** means a step needs the documentation to work out; **low** is polish.

| CA-2 | G-01 checklist | The first-run checklist is the last section, about 1,300 px down, below health causes, the health monitor, actions and journal verification. | high | While any item is open the checklist is the first section; once complete it moves to the end. | CA-15 | Status bar | "live" and the actor form `admin:admin` are terse for a first visit. | low | "live updates" ("no live updates: reload to update" when the stream drops) and the account name alone after the role; the full actor is the name's tooltip. | fixed 2026-10-19 |

Checked and found in order: every empty state seen (Approvals, Sessions, Audit with no match, Research, Data server) names the action or event that fills it, as `console/e2e/states.spec.ts` enforces; no gradients, glass, glow, decorative shadows, large rounded cards, hero layouts or decorative motion; state is carried by a word and a shape as well as color; focus is a visible 2 px outline, the skip link is the first focusable element and focus moves to the view heading; `console/src` contains none of the banned marketing words, exclamation marks or emoji.

## Not changed and why

- **Account radio labels** on the sign-in page: the e2e helpers select accounts by these labels and `docs/console.md` quotes them; the new description under the choice carries the clarification instead.
- **Checklist item names**: they are the item names of SRS FR-8.31, and `console/e2e/checklist.spec.ts` finds rows by them. The terminology pass of 2026-10-19 renamed "First harness connected" to "First agent connected" in FR-8.31, the view and the test together.
